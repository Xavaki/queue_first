Queue First settings

Queue First can't reach YouTube.

Firefox's access to www.youtube.com is switched off for this extension, so nothing is locked or queued.

Then reload any open YouTube tabs.

Queue First works on www.youtube.com in desktop Firefox. YouTube Music, the mobile site, embedded players on other sites, other browsers and devices, and private windows are not covered. It is a voluntary safeguard.
